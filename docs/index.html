Slette kontoen

Åpne Innstillinger → Personvern og data → Slett konto i appen. Kontoen og alle studiedata slettes, både på telefonen og i skyen.

Support

Medora is a study app for medical students, with AI-made quizzes, flashcards and Active Recall per topic, a study plan and progress tracking.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to nima.maleki200214@gmail.com.

Deleting your account

Open Settings → Privacy and data → Delete account in the app. The account and all study data are deleted from the phone and the cloud.
